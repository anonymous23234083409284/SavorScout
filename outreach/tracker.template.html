Campus Paper Pitches

One email for every student newspaper in Savor Scout's college food rankings, plus local news in the towns that came out on top. Work down the list, 15 to 30 a day.

Before you send

  1. Open one ranking, like savorscout.net/rankings/big-ten, to check the pages are live. Every email links to them.
  2. Add your name for the sign-off: Saved on this device
  3. Send each email by itself, from your own address. No BCC and no attachments.
  4. Mark it Sent here. If there's no reply after five days, send the follow-up once, then stop.
  5. If a paper runs the story, ask them to link the ranking page. That link is what helps the site rank.
