User-agent: *
# Every URL with a query string is the app's homepage with a search in it
# (/?near=…, /?craving=…, /?quiz=1, /?ref=…). Each one correctly names the
# homepage as its canonical, so none can rank on its own, and Google filed
# thousands of them under "Alternate page with proper canonical tag" while
# spending crawls on them instead of on the campus, city and guide pages. The
# site's own links now put the search after "#", which creates no new URL;
# this stops the crawling of the ones already discovered or shared. No page
# meant for search results has a query string.
Disallow: /*?

# Pointing at the sitemap here means crawlers find it without anyone having to
# submit it by hand, and it is the one robots.txt directive that actively helps
# rather than just permitting.
Sitemap: https://www.savorscout.net/sitemap.xml
