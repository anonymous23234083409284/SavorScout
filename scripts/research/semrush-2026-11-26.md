## What it means for savorscout.net

- **Late-night is the opening**: high volume at KD 15-22, ranked by small local guides. Our pages for it:
  `/what-to-eat/late-night` (the guide) plus the campus and city pages, which could carry "late night food
  near <campus>" wording. Only say what the data supports: never "open now" unless the page shows real hours.
- **"Cheap eats <city>" is mid-difficulty (KD 23-34) and city-specific**: the matching pages are the city pages
  (`/eat/seattle-wa.html`; Brooklyn and Queens only have the one `/eat/new-york-ny.html`, so borough pages
  are a gap) and `/what-to-eat/on-a-budget`. We have no prices, so a page can say "places near campus" and
  "quick, casual places", never "cheapest".
- **Skip generic "best food near me" terms** (KD 55-79, owned by Maps, Tripadvisor and DoorDash).
- Compare against Search Console before building anything: `python3 scripts/research/keywords.py gsc`.
