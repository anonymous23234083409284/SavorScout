# What the lab has learned

Learned from **34 TikToks** posted 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-10. The model explains about 88% of the differences in views (in-sample, so treat it as a guide). Multipliers below are on views; "clear" means the data shows it repeatedly, "likely" means it leans that way, "unclear" means not enough evidence yet.

**Last 7 days:** 24 videos, median 1,113 views, 13 of 24 at 1,000+.

## What drives views

- **Countdown vs. 'how to tell' tip: x5.7 views** (clear)
- a countdown of named schools (vs. the first week's videos): **x9.15** views (clear, seen in 15 videos)
- a 'how to tell' tip (vs. the first week's videos): **x1.61** views (likely, seen in 5 videos)
- more famous schools on screen (+1 tier): **x2.12** views (likely, seen in 15 videos)
- grouping by university system (UC, Cal State): **x1.80** views (likely, seen in 2 videos)
- one more school in the ranking: **x0.94** views (likely, seen in 15 videos)
- a bigger lead for #1 (x2): **x1.38** views (likely, seen in 15 videos)
- grouping by conference (vs. state): **x1.66** views (likely, seen in 5 videos)
- posting on a weekend: **x1.22** views (unclear, seen in 6 videos)
- naming the upset in the hook ('Hint: it's not X'): **x1.35** views (unclear, seen in 11 videos)
- a more famous school named in the tease (+1 tier): **x1.13** views (unclear, seen in 15 videos)
- posting at 6 or 9 PM (vs. noon or 3 PM): **x0.99** views (unclear, seen in 17 videos)
